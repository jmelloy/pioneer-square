# Background Prompts

Use **DreamShaper XL** or **Juggernaut XL**. Generate at **1344×768**, CFG 6–8, 30–40 steps.

---

## Factory Floor — Main Scene

The primary backdrop behind the workstations, gears, and conveyor belt.

```
pixel art factory workshop interior, wide establishing shot, SNES 16-bit style,
warm amber and gold lighting, deep dark brown stone walls and floor,
copper and brass pipes running along the ceiling with riveted joints,
glowing teal computer terminals and control panels in the background,
a large furnace with orange fire glow on the right side,
riveted metal catwalks and scaffolding, warm lantern light casting long shadows,
cozy industrial atmosphere, Chrono Trigger Lucca's workshop aesthetic,
no characters, game background art, rich warm color palette,
dark browns and oranges with teal accent lighting
```

### Variants to Try

**Daytime / brighter shift:**
```
same as above, add: bright warm morning light through high windows,
dust motes in golden light beams, more amber tones, less shadow
```

**Night shift / moody:**
```
same as above, add: nighttime factory, deep shadows, only lanterns and
teal screen glow for light, dramatic contrast, atmospheric
```
